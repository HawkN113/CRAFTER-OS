# ⚠️ Tactical Warning: Cultural Compatibility

CRAFTER (OS) is a **high-transparency, high-accountability** framework. While it is designed to maximize engineering output, it is not a "politically safe" system. Before deployment, understand that its principles may cause friction in certain environments.

---

## 🛑 The Hierarchy Scandal
*CRAFTER prioritizes Signal over Status.*

* **The Conflict:** In rigid, top-down hierarchies, decisions are often made based on seniority rather than technical logic.
* **The Friction:** CRAFTER’s mandate for **Cognitive Clarity** and **ADRs** requires questioning the "Why." If your organization relies on *"Do it because I said so"* management, this OS will expose systemic incompetence, potentially leading to social or professional conflict.

---

## 🔍 High-Exposure Responsibility
*There is no place to hide in this system.*

* **The Conflict:** Many corporate cultures use "vague requirements" as a safety net to avoid individual blame.
* **The Friction:** By defining a 100% clear **Definition of Done** and documenting every decision, you eliminate plausible deniability. This system is for those who want to be judged by their results, which can be threatening to "low-signal" team members.

---

## 🛡️ The "Focus Shield" vs. "Hero Culture"
*CRAFTER kills the "always-available" myth.*

* **The Conflict:** Some managers measure value by how fast you reply to a Slack ping, not by the complexity of the problems you solve.
* **The Friction:** Implementing a strict **Zero-Noise Zone** (2–4 hours of isolation) may be perceived as *"not being a team player"* in cultures that prioritize performative busyness over actual deep work.

---

## 💎 Radical Integrity
*Truth precedes comfort.*

* **The Warning:** CRAFTER forces you to communicate delays before they happen (**Reputation Principle**). In environments where people prefer "comfortable lies" until the deadline day, your radical transparency might be labeled as "pessimism" or "insubordination."

---

> **⚠️ Deployment Notice:** If your culture values compliance over competence, appearance over outcomes, or consensus over clarity — **do not use CRAFTER.** It will act as an antibody to your environment.
